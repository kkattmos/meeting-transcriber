  error Running as root without --no-sandbox is not supported is from Chrome's
  zygote process — it kills itself before it ever opens a window. The wait in
  the script then returns immediately, the cleanup trap fires, and the whole
  thing unwinds before you can sign in.

  What I fixed

  Added --no-sandbox to the Chrome launch in two places:

  1. first_time_login.sh — added --no-sandbox to the direct Chrome launch. (This

  record_and_transcribe.sh runs the whole pipeline under sudo -H, so the same
  crash would have hit you on the very first meeting right after a successful
  login. Better to fix it once than to debug it later with a half-transcribed
  WAV file.

  This is the standard workaround for running headless Chrome as root in
  containers/VMs, and it's safe here because there's no untrusted content being
  rendered — the VM is your own.
